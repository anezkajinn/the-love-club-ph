About Us

The Love Club is a community dedicated to the appreciation of literature and the art of book collecting. We provide a space for readers to engage in thoughtful discussion, share curated recommendations, and connect over a shared interest in romanticizing reading. Our focus is on fostering a consistent reading habit through monthly selections and organized community events.

The Love Club was founded by Ms. Anezka Jinn as a sanctuary for the hopeless romantics and the dreamers. Born from a love for stories that make the heart ache and soar, this club is a dedicated space for those who find magic in the pages of a book.

We’re so excited to have you! Here is a little glimpse of what to expect when you become a member of our community:

  • The Monthly Pick: A carefully selected book for us to dive into together every month.
  • Curated Treats: Members-only reading kits, aesthetic digital guides, and sweet little surprises.
  • Special Gatherings: Themed reading dates, afternoon picnics, and discussion nights about our current reads.

To keep our sanctuary safe and happy for all our members and future members, we simply ask that you lead with kindness and respect for every unique perspective shared. We maintain a no-pressure environment where you are always welcome regardless of your reading pace, ensuring our space remains a soft, supportive haven for everyone to open their hearts.

LITTLE WOMEN by Louisa May Alcott

The Love Club’s selection of Louisa May Alcott’s Little Women for the month of May is a poetic nod to both the season and the book’s enduring themes of growth and domestic warmth. As spring reaches its peak, the novel’s focus on the “blossoming” of the four March sisters Meg, Jo, Beth, and Amy–mirrors the natural world’s transition into full bloom.
